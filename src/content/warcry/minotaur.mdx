# Minotaur

<ListSection title="Fur">
  1. Start off by give it a nice base coat of Burnt red. 1. Then follow this with a nice light wash of Dark grey. 1.
  Next apply a Highlight to some of the strands of fur with a mix of Burnt red and Heavy Gold brown going over a second
  time to build up the strength. 1. Add more heavy gold brown to the previous mix and then apply this to the edge of the
  fur to intensify the highlights
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Round shoulder plates">
  1. Mix dark gunmetal and a small amount Black together to create the base layer 1. Start applying dark gunmetal 1.
  Apply a nice coat of chipping medium, once this is dry apply two coats of yellow olive 1. Once the two coats of yellow
  olive is dry apply the chipping process by using a damp dry brush to start removing the paint. 1. Apply some thin
  layers of dry rust to parts of the armour plate 1. Next Apply some small amounts of light rust to selected parts of
  the armour plate
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Flat shoulder plate">
  1. Mix Gunmetal and a small amount Black together to create the base layer 1. Do a black out like of all the detail 1.
  Start dry brushing on layers of Tinny tin followed by gunmetal 1. Again reinforce the shadows by outlining all the
  details 1. Hight the tips and edges with Silver
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Head plate">
  1. Mix Gunmetal 72.054 and a small amount Black together to create the base layer 1. Mix a small amount of Silver to
  Gunmetal to build up the layers 1. Follow this with pure silver on the egdes 1. Apply a nice coat of chipping medium,
  once this is dry apply two coats of yellow olive 1. Once the two coats of yellow olive is dry apply the chipping
  process by using a damp brush to start removing the paint. 1. As a final step use some dry rust to tone down the
  colours and drying in line with the rest of the colours schema
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Belly plate">
  1. Give the belly plate a nice even layer of German c. black 1. Stipple some Gunmetal on to create texture and then
  and then layer with more Gunmetal.
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Ridged horns">
  1. Do a base coat of Heavy brown 1. Do a wash of German c. black 1. Build the main colour back up to Heavy brown 1.
  Star doing highlights by adding Iraqui sand to Heavy brown
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Smooth horns">
  1. Do a nice and consistent base coat of Iraqui sand 1. Glaze brown ink to create a stained look starting at the root.
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Head, toe & finger nails">
  1. Base coat of heavy brown 1. Build up the layers with heavy brown and Iraqui Sand 1. Apply detail with pure Iraqui
  sand 1. Apply some glaze of Brown ink 1. Outline with more brown ink where the horns meet the skin
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Leather">
  1. Base coat the area with Chocolate brown 1. Use Flat earth to stain some of the area 1. Use Dark sands for a second
  highlight consertaing on the edges 1. Apply smoke to blend all the colours together
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Axe head">
  1. Coat the axe head in matt varnish 1. Dry brush gunmetal 1. Stipple Silver 1. Apply a wash of violet shade to the
  edge of the blade
</ListSection>
