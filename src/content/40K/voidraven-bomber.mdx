# Voidraven Bomber

<Image src={beastLord} alt="A well painted module." width="359" height="268" loading="eager" />

<ListSection title="Armor">
  1. Undercoat with a mix of both Black & UK Bronze Green 1. Base coat with a mix of both Black & Scurvy Green
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Cockpit & Bomber bay">
  1. After masking off the area base coat with ATM-11 Blue Grey 1. Mix Nato black with ATM-11 Blue Grey to create the
  shade 1. Use USAF Medium grey on the raised parts for a bit of contrast
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Exhaust">
  1. Base coat with Exhaust Manifold 1. Mix in some Black to Exhaust Manifold and apply to the end of the exhaust 1.
  Next apply some Skin wash and Purple tone to the exhaust in a random manner to create heat discolouration
</ListSection>

<ListSection title="Panels">
  1. Begin by outlining all the panels with Emerald 1. Follow this with a mix of Emerald, Sun Yellow and Glacier Blue 1.
  For the final highlight use Emerald and Sun Yellow
</ListSection>
